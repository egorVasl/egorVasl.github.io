About the studio

Vaslix Games is an independent Android publisher founded in 2025. We build small, focused apps across genres — puzzle today, audio and arcade titles next — with a privacy-first stance: no accounts, no proprietary servers storing user data, no in-house analytics. Two titles are live on Google Play: Gridlix:2048 and Blocklix, an 8×8 block puzzle with six difficulty levels, 38 themes and 60 ranks, localised into 12 languages.
