The Daily is seeded from the date, so every player worldwide gets the same board, graded out of three stars; a streak with shields survives a missed evening, and a finished month is worth a medal. How it plays

One gesture, all the way down

There is nothing to learn beyond the swipe. Everything after that is arithmetic and nerve.

  1. 1

    Swipe

    One direction moves every tile on the board at once. There is no partial move.

  2. 2

    Merge

    Two equal tiles that touch become one, worth double. A tile merges once per move.

  3. 3

    Reach the target

    In Endless the goal is your own record. In the Daily it is a stated tile inside a stated number of moves.

The Daily

The same puzzle as everyone else

A dated seed builds the opening position and the whole spawn stream, so the board you are staring at is the board everyone else is staring at.

One seed, one board

The opening is played out in advance by a scripted bot, and its move count sets the par you are measured against. Several attempts a day; the best one counts.

  • ★☆☆

    Reach the target tile inside the move cap.

  • ★★☆

    Do it inside par — the bot's own move count.

  • ★★★

    Inside par with no booster touched all game.

The month

A streak you can actually keep

One missed evening should not erase four months. Shields absorb a miss, and the calendar keeps the whole month in view.

Played Shielded Missed

Shields

A shield is spent automatically on the day you miss, and you earn them back by playing. The streak survives an ordinary bad week.

Monthly medals

Finish a month and it is graded once, kept forever, and shown in the trophy case next to your best streak.

  • Bronze
  • Silver

Boosters

Five, three uses each

The cap is per game, not per day, so a booster is a decision inside a run rather than a resource to hoard.

  • Undo

    Takes back the last move, spawn included.

  • Delete

    Removes one tile you point at.

  • Swap

    Trades the places of two tiles.

  • Cleanup

    Sweeps every tile of one value off the board.

  • Shuffle

    Rearranges what is already there, without adding anything.

For a free player each use is paid with a rewarded video you choose to watch; Premium skips both the video and the counter. Using none is the third Daily star.

Premium

Play without the interruptions

A subscription through Google Play, monthly or yearly, with a free trial on the yearly plan. Cancel from Play at any time.

  • No banners and no interstitials, anywhere
  • Boosters without watching anything first
  • Continues when the board fills up
  • The four locked palettes open up

The Daily, its stars, the streak and the medals work exactly the same whether you pay or not.
