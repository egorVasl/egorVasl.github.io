Privacy Policy

How Vaslix Games handles information across all our Android apps — games and audio/podcast titles. This policy applies the same way to every app we release, regardless of genre or format. No accounts, no servers of our own, no nonsense. Currently covers Gridlix:2048 and Blocklix, both live on Google Play; audio/podcast titles in development.

Last updated: 2026-09-06 GDPR / CCPA / COPPA aware Google Play Billing

The short version

  • What stays on your device

    Game state, settings, best scores, and a cached premium-status flag. None of it reaches a server of ours — Android's own backup may copy it into your own Google account (§1d).

  • What we never see

    Payment details, your Google account email, account IDs, contacts, photos, or location beyond the IP-based estimate ad networks use.

  • Ads vs Premium

    Free users see banner, interstitial and occasional app-open ads via Appodeal mediation (plus Google AdMob in some titles). Premium and the one-time Remove Ads purchase turn those off. Rewarded videos are opt-in for everyone: they play only when you tap a watch-to-earn offer.

  • Device identifiers

    Your Google Advertising ID and Android device ID are read so ad networks can serve and frequency-cap ads, and so Google Play Billing can verify your subscription entitlement. Both IDs are resettable in Android Settings.

01

Information we collect

Our apps do not require account creation and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly. Information falls into two groups.

1a. Stored locally on your device

Each app stores the following in a local Room/SQLite database (or an equivalent local store) on your device. We never upload it to a server of ours; Android’s own backup service may copy it into your personal Google account, as described in 1d:

  • App-specific state — e.g. game board, score, boosters, and continues for puzzle titles; playback position, subscribed feeds, queue, and the offline download cache for podcast/audio titles
  • Best-game history and best score (puzzle titles), or listening history (audio titles), kept as a local-only record
  • App settings: sound on/off, haptics on/off, theme mode, playback speed, and similar per-app preferences
  • A first-launch flag used to decide whether to show the welcome paywall
  • A cached summary of your subscription status — active/inactive, plan, product ID, base plan ID — so the app applies your premium entitlement immediately at launch without waiting for the network

What is not stored locally: payment details, purchase tokens, and Google account identifiers. None of this leaves Google Play.

1b. Collected by third parties on our behalf

The third-party services described below may collect technical and advertising-related information automatically:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) — read so ad networks can deliver ads, frequency-cap them, and attribute installs. While Premium or Remove Ads is active it is not used for banner, interstitial or app-open advertising; it is still processed when you explicitly choose to watch a rewarded video.
  • Android device ID (SSAID) — read on every launch as a stable, app-scoped device identifier. Used by Google Play Billing to verify your subscription entitlement and by the ad mediation SDK for fraud and invalid-traffic signals. It is scoped to this app's signing key and is not linked to your Google account.
  • IP address and approximate geographic location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and time zone
  • App version, session timestamps, and crash diagnostics
  • Aggregated, non-personally-identifying usage events — including podcast/audio playback events (episode started, completed, skipped) for audio titles, used only in aggregate form

Why we disclose this: Google Play's Data safety form requires every read of the Advertising ID and the Android device ID to be declared, regardless of whether the value leaves your device. You can reset the Advertising ID at any time from Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and the Android ID is regenerated when you factory-reset the device.

1c. Analytics, crash reports and remote configuration (Google Firebase)

Some of our apps (currently Blocklix) include Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config. Firebase Analytics collects a random app-instance ID, device and OS information, and aggregated in-app events (for example "game finished", "purchase completed", "ad watched") so we can understand overall usage and balance the game economy; we do not use it to build advertising profiles. Firebase Remote Config downloads a small set of configuration values (feature flags and game-balance numbers) and sends no personal data beyond the technical request metadata. Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes or freezes: the stack trace, the device model, the OS and app version, and a random installation identifier — enough to find the bug, and nothing you typed or played. Details: firebase.google.com/support/privacy ↗.

1d. Backup and cloud save

Android's own backup is enabled for our apps. If backup is turned on for your device, Android copies the local data listed in 1a — board, scores, settings, unlocks — into your personal Google account and restores it when you reinstall on the same account or set up a new device. That copy belongs to you: Android handles and encrypts it, and we can neither read it nor delete it. You control it in Android Settings → Google → Backup. Separately, Blocklix includes an optional cloud save built on Google Play Games Saved Games: one save file in your own Play Games storage, so a run can follow you between devices. It never runs without a Play Games sign-in, it carries game progress only — never subscription state or purchase data — and it is served by Google, not by a server of ours. The feature ships switched off and is being enabled gradually; if you have signed in and would rather not use it, sign out of Play Games in the app.

02

Advertising partners

Our apps may display banner ads, occasional interstitial ads — including app-open ads when returning to the app — and rewarded video ads that you explicitly choose to watch in exchange for an in-game perk (a booster, a continue after game-over, bonus coins and similar). A Premium subscription or the one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off banner, interstitial and app-open ads; rewarded videos remain available to every user and only ever play after you tap their offer. We work with the advertising providers below, used together or independently depending on the app.

Appodeal mediation

We use Appodeal as our primary advertising mediation partner. Appodeal's privacy policy is at appodeal.com/privacy-policy ↗.

Appodeal mediates demand from a large number of advertising networks, and the active set evolves as Appodeal updates its SDK and as we change our build configuration. The current, authoritative list is published by Appodeal:

appodeal.com/partners ↗

Each network on that page links to its own privacy policy. Privacy questions specific to ad personalisation can be raised directly with Appodeal at the same site.

Google AdMob

Some of our apps also serve display, native, or rewarded ads through Google AdMob directly. Google's advertising data practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads ↗, and the Google Privacy Policy is at policies.google.com/privacy ↗. You can opt out of personalised Google ads at adssettings.google.com ↗.

Premium subscribers and Remove-Ads purchasers are not shown banner, interstitial or app-open ads. Rewarded videos are never forced on anyone — free or paying, they start only from an explicit tap on a watch-to-earn offer. Limited initialisation traffic to the ad providers may still occur on app start before the entitlement is resolved.

03

Subscriptions and in-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Each of our apps may offer an optional Premium subscription — with weekly, monthly and yearly auto-renewing plans depending on the title, and an introductory free trial on the yearly plan for eligible new subscribers — that turns off banner, interstitial and app-open ads and unlocks premium features specific to that app (for example x2 coins and a daily gift in puzzle titles, or offline downloads and ad-free playback in podcast/audio titles). Some titles also sell one-time products: coin packs, a starter bundle, a piggy-bank payout, and a permanent Remove Ads unlock with the same ad scope as Premium. Everything is sold and processed by Google through Google Play Billing.

  1. All payment, billing, account, and tax information is collected and processed by Google. We do not see, store, or transmit your payment method, your Google account email, or any government identifiers.
  2. The app receives only a non-identifying entitlement signal from Google: whether an active subscription exists for your installation (product ID and base plan ID), and which one-time products have been purchased. This signal is cached locally as described in §1a.
  3. Subscription management, cancellation, refund requests, and renewal dates are handled entirely on Google Play. The "Manage" button inside the app deep-links to the Google Play subscriptions page.
  4. Google Play's privacy policy applies to all billing-related processing: policies.google.com/privacy ↗

04

Legal basis and consent (GDPR / EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the legal basis for advertising-related processing is your consent, collected via an IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF) dialog presented when you first launch the app. You may withdraw or change your consent at any time — via the in-app privacy settings where the app provides them, or by clearing the app's data (Settings → Apps), which presents the consent dialog again on the next launch. The legal basis for processing subscription data via Google Play Billing is the performance of the contract you enter into when purchasing a subscription.

05

CCPA / US state privacy laws

If you are a California resident, or in another applicable US state, you have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for behavioral advertising. The in-app consent dialog provides this opt-out where required. Subscription processing by Google Play is not a "sale" of personal information.

06

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Premium subscription is intended for adult purchasers; in-app purchases require a Google Play account with the appropriate purchase permissions.

07

Data retention

Locally stored data described in §1a is retained on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We do not retain personal information on our own servers. Copies made by Android's own backup service — and a Play Games cloud save, if you enabled it — live in your own Google account: remove them from Android Settings → Google → Backup, and from your Play Games account respectively. The advertising partners and Google Play retain data per their own policies.

08

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we do not directly hold this data, requests should be directed to the advertising partner concerned, or — for billing data — to Google Play.

To remove all locally stored data for any of our apps, uninstall the app or clear its storage from your device's settings (Settings → Apps → [the app] → Storage → Clear data). That clears the copy on the device; a backup Android has already taken is removed under Android Settings → Google → Backup, and a Play Games cloud save from your Play Games account.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example when we add new titles, change advertising partners, or update subscription mechanics. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page indicates when the most recent change was made. Material changes will be reflected in-app on next launch.
