The short version

Why the apps stay small

Vaslix Games is one person. That is not a hardship story — it is the constraint that decides everything else. A studio of one cannot maintain a login system, a server fleet and a support desk, so it does not build them, and the games are better for it.

What is left is the part players actually touch: the board, the timing, the feel of a piece landing. Every hour that would have gone into an account flow goes into the difficulty curve instead.

The catalogue grows slowly and on purpose. A title ships when it is finished, gets updates for years, and never quietly turns into a service you have to log into.

Principles

Four rules that do not move

They are cheaper to keep than to explain away, and they are the reason the apps stay small.

  • No accounts

    Nothing to sign up for. Your progress never leaves the phone it was made on.

  • No payment data stored

    Google Play takes the payment. Your card never reaches us, because it never passes through us.

  • GDPR · CCPA · COPPA

    Consent before the first ad loads, an opt-out where the law asks for one, and a policy that names every company involved.

  • Premium removes ads

    Premium switches off banners, interstitials and app-open ads. Rewarded videos stay available to everyone and start only when you tap one. That is the whole deal.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel from Google Play → Subscriptions, or use the "Manage" button inside the app — it deep-links to the same page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
What happens to my game progress if I uninstall?
Progress lives on your device. In Blocklix, Android’s own backup copies it into your Google account, so reinstalling on the same account usually brings it back; in Gridlix:2048 that backup is switched off, so there a save is lost on uninstall unless you use the Play Games cloud save. We never upload a save to a server of ours. Premium restores by itself once you reinstall and sign in to the same Google account.
Who reads the Advertising ID?
Not us. Our own code never touches it — the advertising SDKs inside the app do, so that ads can be frequency-capped and installs attributed. With Premium, or the Remove Ads purchase in Blocklix, it is no longer used for banners, interstitials or app-open ads; it is still processed if you choose to watch a rewarded video. The Privacy Policy names every SDK that reads it.
Which Android versions are supported?
Android 8.0 (API 26) and up. That is on purpose: a phone handed down twice should still run these.
Which languages do the apps support?
Blocklix ships in 12: English, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Arabic and Simplified Chinese. Gridlix:2048 covers a smaller set. The system language is auto-detected on first launch and can be overridden in app settings.
I found a bug or want to suggest a feature.
Write to vaslixofficial@protonmail.com with a short description. Screenshots and device model help a lot. We read every message.
Will there ever be an iOS version?
Not on the roadmap. One person can keep two Android games updated for years; two platforms would mean neither of them got that. If the catalogue grows enough to change the arithmetic, it will be said here.
Does Premium change how the game plays?
No. Premium removes banners and interstitials, doubles the stars a run pays and adds a daily gift and the premium themes. Every level, rank and mission is the same for everyone.
