How it plays

Three moves you already know

Blocklix is the 8×8 drop-and-clear you have seen before — tuned until every one of these feels right in the hand.

  1. 1

    Drop

    Three pieces at a time. Any of them can go anywhere it fits; nothing rotates, nothing falls.

  2. 2

    Clear

    Fill a row or a column and it vanishes. Fill both at once and the board tells you so.

  3. 3

    Combo

    Clear on consecutive drops and the multiplier climbs. One quiet tick warns you when a line is about to go.

38 themes

Every board wears a palette

Four are yours from install. The rest are earned with stars or come with Premium — and each one changes the sky, the blocks and the way a cleared line lights up.

Blocklix Original Tap a theme to dress the board

Six difficulty levels

Pick the length of your game

Only the piece generator changes. Scoring, boards and ranks stay the same on every rung — harder just means the pieces stop helping you.

  1. Easy

    Long, relaxed games. Fewer stars.

  2. Normal

    The classic pace — about ten minutes.

  3. Hard

    Bigger pieces show up sooner.

  4. Expert

    Every new set of pieces is a puzzle.

  5. Master

    Only the sharpest survive. No easy pieces.

  6. Insane

    Pure luck of the draw. Pieces might not fit at all.

Higher rungs pay more stars per point. Easy pays fewer, and never pretends otherwise.

Boosters

Four ways out of a bad board

Each one is bought with stars you earned by playing. None of them can be bought with money on its own.

  • Hammer

    Smashes any one block

  • Bomb

    Clears a 3×3 area instantly

  • Lightning

    Zaps a full row and column in one strike

  • Shuffle

    Swaps your pieces for three new ones

Stars come from finished games, missions and ranks. Premium doubles what a run pays; it does not sell the boosters directly.

Premium

What money changes — and what it never touches

Premium removes what gets in the way and speeds up what you already earn. It does not unlock a level, a rank or a mission.

  • Banners, interstitials and app-open ads switch off
  • A run pays twice the stars
  • A daily gift, and the premium themes
  • Every difficulty, rank and mission stays exactly as it was

Rewarded videos stay opt-in for everyone: they only play when you tap the button that asks for one.
