Principles

Four rules that do not move

They are cheaper to keep than to explain away, and they are the reason the apps stay small.

  • No accounts

    No sign-up. Your progress stays on your device.

  • No payment data stored

    Payments run through Google Play. We never see your card.

  • GDPR · CCPA · COPPA

    Consent, opt-outs, and data rights, aligned to the standards.

  • Premium removes ads

    Banners, interstitials and app-open ads switch off for Premium and Remove Ads. Rewarded videos stay opt-in for everyone — they only play when you tap one.
