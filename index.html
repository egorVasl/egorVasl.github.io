FAQ

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel from Google Play → Subscriptions, or use the "Manage" button inside the app — it deep-links to the same page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

What happens to my game progress if I uninstall?

Progress lives on your device — uninstalling clears it. We never upload your save to our servers, so there's no cloud backup. Premium status restores automatically once you reinstall and sign in to the same Google account.

Why do you ask for the Advertising ID?

Only on free / non-Premium installs, and only so ad networks can frequency-cap and serve relevant ads. Premium subscribers' Advertising ID is not sent to any ad network. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and above. We target older devices on purpose so the games are still playable on hand-me-down phones.

Which languages do the apps support?

English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French and Simplified Chinese. The system language is auto-detected on first launch and can be overridden in app settings.

I found a bug or want to suggest a feature.

Write to vaslixofficial@protonmail.com with a short description. Screenshots and device model help a lot. We read every message.

Will there ever be an iOS version?

Not on the current roadmap. We're a one-person studio and Android lets us ship and update faster. We may revisit once the catalogue grows.
