How it plays

Three moves you already know

Blocklix is the 8×8 drop-and-clear you have seen before — tuned until every one of these feels right in the hand.

01

Drop

Three pieces at a time. Any of them can go anywhere it fits; nothing rotates, nothing falls.

02

Clear

Fill a row or a column and it vanishes. Fill both at once and the board tells you so.

03
×3

Combo

Clear on consecutive drops and the multiplier climbs. One quiet tick warns you when a line is about to go.

38 themes

Every board wears a palette

Four are yours from install. The rest are earned with stars or come with Premium — and each one changes the sky, the blocks and the way a cleared line lights up.

Blocklix Original1 of 38

Tap a theme to dress the board

Six difficulty levels

Pick the length of your game

Only the piece generator changes. Scoring, boards and ranks stay the same on every rung — harder just means the pieces stop helping you.

Higher rungs pay more stars per point. Easy pays fewer, and never pretends otherwise.

The long game

A road, not a treadmill

Everything below is earned by playing. Nothing here is sold.

60 ranks

Fifteen families of four. Points are your score, weighted by the difficulty you chose.

21 achievement chains

Five steps each, from the first clear to the ones almost nobody reaches.

Daily and weekly missions

Thirteen daily kinds that scale with your rank, two weekly ones, and a chest for the week you keep at it.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Premium change how the game plays?

No. Premium removes banners and interstitials, doubles the stars a run pays and adds a daily gift and the premium themes. Every level, rank and mission is the same for everyone.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel from Google Play → Subscriptions, or use the "Manage" button inside the app — it deep-links to the same page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

What happens to my game progress if I uninstall?

Progress lives on your device, and Android's own backup service copies it into your Google account — so reinstalling on the same account usually brings it back. We never upload your save to a server of ours. Blocklix also has an optional cloud save on Google Play Games, rolling out gradually. Premium restores automatically once you reinstall and sign in to the same Google account.

Why do you ask for the Advertising ID?

So ad networks can frequency-cap and serve relevant ads, and attribute installs. With Premium or the Remove Ads purchase it is no longer used for banners, interstitials or app-open ads; it is still processed if you choose to watch a rewarded video. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and above. We target older devices on purpose so the games are still playable on hand-me-down phones.

Which languages do the apps support?

Blocklix ships in 12: English, Russian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Arabic and Simplified Chinese. Gridlix:2048 covers a smaller set. The system language is auto-detected on first launch and can be overridden in app settings.

I found a bug or want to suggest a feature.

Write to vaslixofficial@protonmail.com with a short description. Screenshots and device model help a lot. We read every message.

Will there ever be an iOS version?

Not on the current roadmap. We're a one-person studio and Android lets us ship and update faster. We may revisit once the catalogue grows.
